An actively maintained FoundryVTT system for running Blades in the Dark, built after running into rough edges with existing sheet implementations.
  • The need: character and crew sheets for Blades in the Dark that actually match how the system plays at the table.
  • The solution: a full FoundryVTT game system with custom actor sheets for both characters and crews.
  • Shipped with tagged releases and an active download/update history.

Source and releases: github.com/Achoobert/foundryvtt_blades68
