A set of small FoundryVTT add-ons built to fix specific pain points from running 200+ paid tabletop sessions.
  • CoC7 Hero Builder: a fullscreen, resumable character creation wizard for the Call of Cthulhu system, writes progress straight to the Actor so players never lose a half-finished investigator.
  • Simple Requests: lets players request to speak or ask for a quick dice roll without the awkward pause of unmuting over voice chat.
  • Mothership PDF Importer: parses Mothership RPG stat block PDFs and imports them directly into Foundry.
  • Token Namer: a small GM addon that auto-names and numbers tokens as they're dropped onto the scene, so a pile of identical monster tokens doesn't turn into "Goblin", "Goblin", "Goblin".
